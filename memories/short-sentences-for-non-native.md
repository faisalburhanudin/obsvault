Use short sentences and plain words in all replies. Avoid long clauses, dashes stacking many ideas into one line, and rare vocabulary.

**Why:** The user is not a native English speaker and asked for text that is easy to read.

**How to apply:** One idea per sentence. Break long explanations into bullets. Keep technical terms (file paths, symbol names) exact, but keep the prose around them simple.
